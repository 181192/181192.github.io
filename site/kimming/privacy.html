Kimming: privacy

Kimming is a personal, non-commercial app with one user: me. It shows my own accounts in one place and forecasts the months ahead.

What data it handles

Balances and transactions from my own bank accounts, read through Enable Banking's account information API with my consent given by BankID. Kimming only reads; it cannot make payments.

Where the data goes

From Enable Banking straight to my phone, where it is stored in an encrypted database. There is no server, no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is shared with anyone. Questions asked in the app are answered by a language model that runs on the phone.

How long it is kept

Until I delete it in the app or remove the app. Consent to read from a bank lasts at most 180 days and can be withdrawn at any time in the app or in the bank.
