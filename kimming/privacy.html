Kimming: privacy

Kimming is a personal, non-commercial app. It shows your own accounts in one place and forecasts the months ahead. Everything stays on your phone.

What data it handles

Balances and transactions from your own bank accounts, read through Enable Banking's account information API with the consent you give by BankID. Each person uses their own Enable Banking account and application, with a key made on their own phone. Kimming only reads; it cannot make payments.

Where the data goes

From Enable Banking straight to your phone, where it is stored in an encrypted database. There is no server, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing is sent to me or anyone else, and I cannot see your data. Questions asked in the app are answered by a language model that runs on the phone. After a bank login, the address kalli.no/kimming/cb normally opens the app directly; if the browser shows that page instead, it only displays the address for you to copy and sends it nowhere.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Consent to read from a bank lasts at most 180 days and can be withdrawn at any time in the app or in the bank.
